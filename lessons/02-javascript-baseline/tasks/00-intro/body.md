### How can I participate?

Basically, you can choose whether you just want to watch the videos first or actively work along right away.
In addition to the content, the solutions to the exercises are also explained.

You can find the [code](https://github.com/workshops-de/javascript-baseline#javascript-baseline) for this lesson [here on GitHub](https://github.com/workshops-de/javascript-baseline#javascript-baseline).
The [README](https://github.com/workshops-de/javascript-baseline#javascript-baseline) contains all the information you need to work locally on your development system.

### What if I don’t finish this course before the workshop starts?

See this course as a reference. It’s not a big deal if you don’t know some concepts.
If you notice during the workshop that you’re unfamiliar with one of the topics mentioned here — no problem.
Take the code as given for now and come back to this video course later to catch up at your own learning pace. 🤗
